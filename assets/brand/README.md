# OpenFrequency brand marks

Copied unchanged from Teder's `assets/brand` (the source of truth for the family): the glyphs
only, drawn inline on their coloured tile by `src/web/html.js`.

| Mark | Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenFrequency | #2747D6 | Halftone, dots grow toward the center |
| Teder (תדר) | #6A3FD6 | One wave, like a tilde |
| Toran (תורן) | #0F7A54 | Three "typing..." dots |

Rules: keep the colors exact, never stretch or recolor the mark, keep at least 1/8 of the icon
size as clear space. `openfrequency-glyph-small.svg` is for 48px and below, where the full
halftone's smallest dots vanish.
